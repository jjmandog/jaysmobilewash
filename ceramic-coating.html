What is Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that is applied by hand to the exterior of a vehicle. It chemically bonds with the vehicle's factory paint, creating a layer of protection. It's a semi-permanent solution that provides a high-gloss finish and protects your paint from environmental contaminants, UV rays, and minor scratches.

Key Benefits:

  • Long-Lasting Protection: Shields your paint from bird droppings, bug splatter, and road grime.
  • Enhanced Gloss: Creates a deep, reflective shine that is superior to wax.
  • Hydrophobic Properties: Water and rain bead up and roll off the surface, taking dirt with them.
  • Easier Cleaning: The slick surface prevents dirt from sticking, making washing your car easier.
  • UV Ray Protection: Prevents paint from oxidizing and fading.

Our Ceramic Coating Process

1. Decontamination Wash

We perform a thorough hand wash and use a clay bar to remove all surface contaminants.

2. Paint Correction

We perform a single-stage paint correction to remove minor swirls and imperfections for a flawless base.

3. Coating Application

The ceramic coating is meticulously applied by hand to every painted surface of your vehicle.

4. Curing

The coating cures for several hours, forming a hard, protective layer. We use an infrared lamp to accelerate this process.
