Why Clay Bar + De-Iron Before Any Wax or Sealant?

Without Proper Prep:

  • Wax/sealant bonds to contaminants, not paint
  • Embedded brake dust and iron particles remain
  • Rough surface prevents proper adhesion
  • Protection fails prematurely
  • Wasted time and money

With Clay Bar + De-Iron:

  • Ultra-smooth, contaminant-free surface
  • Maximum bonding to clean paint
  • Full 6-month protection duration
  • Superior water beading and protection
  • Professional results that last

That's why our SiO2 Sealant service ALWAYS includes clay bar and de-iron preparation!
